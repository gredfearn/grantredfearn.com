# Welcome!

I built this website as a place to bring together the things I’m most passionate
about. Technical writing, astrophotography, and music all in one corner of
the internet. It’s equal parts portfolio, notebook, and creative outlet.

Professionally, I’m deeply interested in modern infrastructure, emerging
technologies, and the constantly evolving world of DevOps and platform
engineering. Over the past 10 years, I’ve worked extensively with Kubernetes at
scale, building and maintaining resilient platforms, improving developer
workflows, and automating just about everything that can be automated. Outside
of engineering, I’m equally drawn to creative work. Astrophotography gives me a
reason to slow down and look up, while music has always been a way to
experiment, explore, and build something personal. This site is where all of
those interests intersect.

## Blog

Most of the writing you’ll find here leans technical in nature. Some posts may
be deep dives into how I configure my Nix environment, lessons learned from
building Kubernetes operators, or small workflow improvements and automation
tricks that make life easier. Other times, I may write about audio production
techniques, home lab experiments, or whatever new technology has captured my
attention lately.

## Astrophotography

Beyond the technical side, I’ll also be sharing stories and photos from my dark
sky adventures. Astrophotography has become one of my favorite ways to
disconnect, explore remote places, and spend time under truly incredible skies.
This blog is a place for all of it, both the engineering challenges and the
creative pursuits that keep me inspired.

## Music

In a previous life, I worked as a post-production audio engineer, producer, and
DJ. Even after moving into the world of infrastructure and platform engineering,
music has remained a constant creative outlet in my life. I still spend a lot of
my free time writing, recording, and experimenting with sound, usually late at
night and often a little obsessively.

For me, making music is a way to balance out the highly technical nature of
engineering work. It exercises a completely different part of the brain and
gives me space to explore ideas without worrying about scalability, reliability,
or production incidents. The music here is personal, experimental, and always
evolving, much like everything else I create.

Thank you for stopping by! Stay tuned for more!
